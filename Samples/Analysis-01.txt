PHISHING EMAIL ANALYSIS 01
==========================

Classification: PHISHING

Email Type: Amazon-style Account Alert

PHISHING INDICATORS
-------------------

1. Sender Domain
The sender uses a suspicious domain that attempts to look like an Amazon security address.

2. Urgency
The email gives the recipient only 24 hours to respond.

3. Threatening Language
It claims the account may be permanently suspended.

4. Generic Greeting
The email starts with "Dear Customer" instead of using the recipient's name.

5. Suspicious Link
The email provides a link to an unfamiliar account-verification domain.

6. Information Request
The recipient is encouraged to verify account information through the provided link.

ANALYST ASSESSMENT
------------------

The email uses urgency, fear of account suspension, and a suspicious sender domain to encourage the recipient to click a verification link. These are common phishing indicators.

RECOMMENDED ACTION
------------------

Do not click the link or provide account information.

Access the service through the official Amazon application or by manually entering the known official website address.
